    Tell the association what you think of Covenants 1 and 19

    The covenant rewrite is open for owner comment and no deadline has been posted. Two changes affect every owner. Covenant 1 would ban all outdoor fires without the association's written approval, which could cover gas fire tables, fire pits, outdoor fireplaces, and grills, and an approval could be revoked at any time. Covenant 19 adds an undefined test that activities, amenities, and lighting be consistent with a single-family home on a comparably sized lot, and lets the association set quiet hours for noise and lighting without an owner vote.

    1. Open the feedback form. Each covenant has its own comment box.
    2. Covenant 1 box: ask that outdoor fire be defined as burning wood, brush, leaves, or trash, and campfires; that gas fire features and grills be expressly allowed; and that an approval be revocable only for cause or during an official burn ban.
    3. Covenant 19 box: ask that the sentence about being consistent with a single-family home be removed, that quiet hours be set only after owner notice and comment, and that entry, path, and stair safety lighting be exempt.
    4. Use your own words. Short comments from many owners carry more weight than one long letter.
    5. The form asks for your name, Massanutten address, and email.

  • CountyNewDeadline Thu Dec 31 · 73 days left

    Existing short-term rentals: apply for your county permit by December 31

    Under the ordinance adopted December 10, 2025, short-term rentals already operating in Rockingham County must apply for a county permit by December 31, 2026.

    1. Read the requirements on the county's Short-Term Rentals page.
    2. Download the application (updated October 2, 2026) and the affidavit of compliance from that page.
    3. Apply well before the deadline in case the county asks for more information.
