Massanutten covenants

Owners should shape the covenant rewrite

The association is rewriting the covenants every owner lives under. No comment deadline or vote process has been posted, so comment now while changes are still possible.

Before the task force meets Thu Nov 5, in 17 days

Tell the association what you think of Covenants 1 and 19

Covenant 1 would ban outdoor fires without written approval. Covenant 19 adds new limits on activities, lighting, and quiet hours. No deadline is posted.

Open the feedback form
What to write
  1. Open the feedback form. Each covenant has its own comment box.
  2. Covenant 1 box: ask that outdoor fire be defined as burning wood, brush, leaves, or trash, and campfires; that gas fire features and grills be expressly allowed; and that an approval be revocable only for cause or during an official burn ban.
  3. Covenant 19 box: ask that the sentence about being consistent with a single-family home be removed, that quiet hours be set only after owner notice and comment, and that entry, path, and stair safety lighting be exempt.
  4. Use your own words. Short comments from many owners carry more weight than one long letter.
  5. The form asks for your name, Massanutten address, and email.

More ways to help

  • Go to the Covenant Amendment Task Force meeting on November 5Meeting Thu Nov 5, in 17 days
