Work with rental owners, in public

Owners are asking the Board of Supervisors to treat short-term rental owners as stakeholders: talk with us, and set rental rules in public meetings with time for comment.

Board meets Wed Oct 28, today

Ask the Board of Supervisors to work with rental owners in public

Email the Board before it meets October 28. Ask that owners be treated as stakeholders and that rental rules be set in public, with time for comment.

Contact the Board
What to say
  1. Massanutten is in District 5, represented by Supervisor Joel Hensley. The Board page lists all five supervisors and has an Email Us link. Write to all five.
  2. Say who you are: a Massanutten owner who rents short term, and whether you live here.
  3. Ask for three things: meet with rental owners, put any change to rental rules on a public agenda, and allow public comment before a vote.
  4. Keep it short and courteous, in your own words.
  5. The October 28 agenda has no open public comment period, and speakers at its hearings must stay on the item being heard, so email is the way to be heard this time.

More ways to help

  • Existing short-term rentals: apply for your county permit by December 31Deadline Thu Dec 31, in 64 days

    Every short-term rental already operating must apply by December 31. The form asks for your bedrooms and the most guests you plan to host.

    1. Download the application from the county's Short-Term Rentals page.
    2. The form allows up to two guests per bedroom, plus four on public sewer.
    3. Attach a site layout, a fire safety and evacuation plan with a diagram for each bedroom, proof of short-term rental liability insurance, and the fee.
    4. The affidavit of compliance on that page is for renewing next year, not for this application.
    5. Apply early in case the county asks for more information.

What we found, last 30 days

  • Board denied the 10-bedroom short-term rental barn, 5 to 0Wed Oct 14

    On October 14 the Board denied a permit to turn a barn on Resort Drive, McGaheysville, into a second dwelling with up to 10 bedrooms for short-term rental. Supervisor Hensley, who represents Massanutten, made the motion; he said the application left out maximum occupancy and that second dwellings on farm land were meant for family or farming. Chair Longcor and Supervisor Dale called the rental a commercial business in a non-commercial zone. Neighbors spoke against it. Source: the October 14 minutes in the October 28 agenda packet.

  • Another approval came with a ban on short-term rentalsWed Oct 14

    Also on October 14, the Board approved a second dwelling in Cross Keys for an aging parent, with the condition that neither house may ever be a short-term rental. Supervisor Dale proposed the condition. Supervisor Wolfe-Garrison objected that it limits property rights and sets a precedent, but the vote was 5 to 0.
