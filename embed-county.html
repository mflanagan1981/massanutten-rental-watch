Work with rental owners, in public

Owners are asking the Board of Supervisors to treat short-term rental owners as stakeholders: talk with us, and set rental rules in public meetings with time for comment.

Board meets Wed Oct 28, in 6 days

Ask the Board of Supervisors to work with rental owners in public

Email the Board, or speak at its October 28 meeting. Ask that owners be treated as stakeholders and that rental rules be set in public, with time for comment.

Contact the Board
What to say
  1. Massanutten is in District 5, represented by Supervisor Joel Hensley. The Board page lists all five supervisors and has an Email Us link.
  2. Say who you are: a Massanutten owner who rents short term, and whether you live here.
  3. Ask for three things: meet with rental owners, put any change to rental rules on a public agenda, and allow public comment before a vote.
  4. Keep it short and courteous, in your own words.
  5. To speak in person: 6 PM at 20 East Gay Street, Harrisonburg. The agenda posts the Friday before; check it for the public comment period.

More ways to help

  • Existing short-term rentals: apply for your county permit by December 31Deadline Thu Dec 31, in 70 days

    Every short-term rental already operating must apply by December 31. The form asks for your bedrooms and the most guests you plan to host.

    1. Download the application from the county's Short-Term Rentals page.
    2. The form allows up to two guests per bedroom, plus four on public sewer.
    3. Attach a site layout, a fire safety and evacuation plan with a diagram for each bedroom, proof of short-term rental liability insurance, and the fee.
    4. The affidavit of compliance on that page is for renewing next year, not for this application.
    5. Apply early in case the county asks for more information.
