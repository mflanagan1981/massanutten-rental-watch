Rockingham County

Work with rental owners, in public

Owners are asking the Board of Supervisors to treat short-term rental owners as stakeholders: talk with us, and set rental rules in public meetings with time for comment.

Deadline Thu Dec 31, in 49 days

Apply for your county rental permit by December 31: a complete application is what counts

At the November 12 work session, staff said rentals that apply by the deadline would keep operating under the new zoning rules. The deadline is still December 31. A possible extension to April 1 goes to the Board on December 9, so treat December 31 as real.

County short-term rental page
What you need
  1. Download the application from the county's Short-Term Rentals page.
  2. The form allows up to two guests per bedroom, plus four on public sewer. Under the draft, what your permit approves is what continues, so enter the occupancy you actually need, up to that limit.
  3. Attach a site layout, a fire safety and evacuation plan with a diagram for each bedroom, proof of short-term rental liability insurance, and the $200 application fee.
  4. The affidavit of compliance on that page is for renewing next year, not for this application.
  5. Apply now rather than in December. Call the county's short-term rental administrator at (540) 564-3029 to confirm your application is complete, and ask for your approved use and occupancy in writing.

  • Staff: rentals permitted by the deadline would keep operating under the new zoningThu Nov 12New

    Staff said rentals that obtain the county permit by the deadline would continue, whatever the new zoning says for their district. The recreational lodge rule (special use permit and commercial building standards above 10 guests) would apply to new rentals, new construction and homes converting to rental. Grandfathered homes still must meet septic, building, fire and parking requirements. Whether that status passes to a buyer was not answered. Staff also said the Virginia building code itself does not set the 10-guest line.

    Source

  • Draft zoning: new rentals by right only in A-1, A-2, RV and R-4; Massanutten stays by rightThu Nov 12New

    Under the draft, new short-term rentals in single-family homes would be allowed by right only in the A-1, A-2, RV and R-4 (Massanutten) districts, need a special use permit in RR-1, and be prohibited in R-1, R-2, R-3 and the planned residential districts. Duplexes and rowhouses would be allowed only in R-4, at two guests per bedroom. Owner-occupied rentals would be allowed everywhere, with a 90-day yearly limit when the owner is away.

  • Permit deadline stays December 31 for now; April 1 extension to be considered December 9Thu Nov 12New

    Supervisors split on extending the deadline. As discussion ended, an extension to April 1, 2027 appeared to have enough support to come back for consideration at the December 9 meeting, but no vote was taken. Staff said about 84 permits were approved and 21 were in review as of November 5, out of an estimated 700 or more rentals. A complete application by the deadline is what counts.

  • Draft county zoning rules: over 10 guests would need a special permit and commercial constructionFri Nov 6

    The packet for the November 12 work session proposes adding short-term rentals to the zoning ordinance. A rental with more than 10 guests becomes a "recreational lodge," allowed only by special use permit in the A-1, A-2, RV, RR-1, and R-4 districts and built to commercial code. Homes that already hold the county permit may continue; homes without one by the deadline would need the special use permit and commercial upgrades to host more than 10. Rentals of 10 or fewer stay allowed by right in A-1, A-2, RV, and R-4 (Massanutten's covenants describe its lots as zoned R-4), need a special use permit in RR-1, and are barred in R-1, R-2, R-3, and R-5 neighborhoods unless the owner lives there. A rental may include only one dwelling unit, so no renting a second living space in the same house. Owner-occupied homes can rent without limit while the owner is present and up to 90 days a year when away. Septic homes would be limited to two guests per bedroom unless the health department approves more. The permit deadline is listed as a topic for discussion.

    Source

  • Draft came from a Land Use Committee that met in June and SeptemberFri Nov 6

A search of the county website finds no notice, agenda, or minutes for those meetings.

  • Board denied the 10-bedroom short-term rental barn, 5 to 0Wed Oct 14

    On October 14 the Board denied a permit to turn a barn on Resort Drive, McGaheysville, into a second dwelling with up to 10 bedrooms for short-term rental.
